# Playbook: Publish — the content pipeline

Every piece of content walks this pipeline in order. No skipping steps.

## 1. Brief
Seat: Content Strategist.
- Pillar, format, hook, CTA, slot on the calendar.

## 2. Create
Seats: Scriptwriter (words), Visual Director (visual brief), Video Editor
(assembly).
- Script/caption drafted in the persona's voice.
- Visuals generated against the master reference, never prompt-only.

## 3. Continuity review — GATE
Seat: Visual Director. **BLOCKING.**
- Same face as master reference? Tattoos correct? Palette only?
- Hands correct, fully in frame on close-ups? Photorealistic?
- Any no → regenerate. Log the fix.

## 4. Voice QA
Seat: Scriptwriter.
- Does it sound like the persona? Short, warm, direct? No AI tells?

## 5. Fact-check — GATE
Seat: Fact-Checker. **BLOCKING.**
- Every technical/factual claim verified against a current source.
- Unverifiable → cut or reframe as opinion.

## 6. Compliance — GATE
Seat: Compliance Officer. **BLOCKING.**
- AI disclosure intact, no deceptive framing, endorsements labeled,
  platform policy clean.

## 7. Schedule & post
Seat: Publishing Manager.
- Caption, hashtags (max 5, rotated), cover, slot. Post or schedule.

## 8. Engage
Seat: Engagement Manager.
- First-hour engagement: reply to early comments, run the comment-CTA
  loop, deliver lead magnets via DM.

## 9. Log
Seat: Analytics Lead.
- Record the post: slot, format, hook, CTA. Feeds the weekly scorecard.
