# Playbook: Analytics — weekly review

Seats: Analytics Lead (runs it), Growth Hacker, Content Strategist, Vesper.
Cadence: weekly. The office never flies blind.

## 1. Scorecard
Pull per-post: saves, shares, reach, follows, comments, profile visits.
Rank the week's posts best → worst.

## 2. Read the signals
- What do the top 3 have in common? (Format? Hook? Slot? Pillar?)
- What do the bottom 3 have in common?
- Follower growth vs. last week. Anything anomalous?

## 3. Experiments
- Growth Hacker's backlog: timing slots (9am/1pm/7pm ET), hook variants,
  CTA variants, format bets.
- One experiment per variable. Run it, read it, log the result.

## 4. Decisions
- Double down on one winner. Kill one loser. Test one new thing.
- Logged as decisions with dates. The calendar updates accordingly.

## 5. Report
One short brief to the owner: what won, what lost, what changes.
Numbers, not vibes.
