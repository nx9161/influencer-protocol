# Playbook: Intake — onboard a new persona

Goal: take a persona from idea to a launch-ready account.

## Phase 1 — Identity
Seats: Vesper, Content Strategist, Partnerships Manager (positioning input).
- Define the persona: name, age, origin, niche, occupation.
- Write the character bible: appearance block (prompt-verbatim), wardrobe
  palette, voice, backstory, content pillars, consistency rules.
- Lock the disclosure stance: openly virtual from day one.

## Phase 2 — Face lock
Seats: Visual Director (blocking), Content Strategist.
- Generate face options from the bible. Owner picks the winner.
- The winner becomes the master reference. Every future generation uses it.
- Save the reference path in the bible. Retire the losers.

## Phase 3 — Profile kit
Seats: Scriptwriter, Content Strategist, Compliance Officer.
- Handle (ranked choices), display name, bio (≤150 chars, disclosure in it),
  profile picture (1080px, face-forward).
- Story highlights plan, first 9-grid with captions.
- Compliance reviews the bio for disclosure before anything goes live.

## Phase 4 — Launch
Seats: Publishing Manager, Engagement Manager, Analytics Lead.
- Owner creates the account, sets handle/bio/pic, connects it to the office.
- Publish the first 9 on the calendar. Engagement Manager works the
  comment-CTA loop from post one.
- Analytics Lead baselines the account: day-7 scorecard.

## Exit criteria
Account live, 9 posts up, disclosure in place, baseline recorded.
