# Playbook: War Room — plan a campaign or content sprint

Session name: "War Room Phase N — Project: X".

## Depth
- **Huddle** (default): one question, 3–5 seats, fast.
- **Full war room**: launches, campaigns, rebrands. All relevant seats.

## Phase 1 — Brief
Seats: Vesper, Content Strategist.
- The objective in one sentence. The audience. The time window.
- What does success look like, in numbers?

## Phase 2 — Angles
Seats: Content Strategist, Scriptwriter, Trend Scout, Growth Hacker.
- 3–5 content angles. Trend Scout brings what's winning in the niche now.
- Growth Hacker attaches the loop: CTA mechanic, lead magnet, experiment.

## Phase 3 — Production plan
Seats: Visual Director, Video Editor, Publishing Manager.
- Shot/asset list per angle. Reference-lock plan for every visual.
- Calendar slots, owners, deadlines.

## Phase 4 — Trust review
Seats: Fact-Checker (blocking), Compliance Officer (blocking).
- Every claim verified. Every disclosure in place. No block = no ship.

## Phase 5 — Sign-off
Seats: Vesper.
- The plan, the owners, the dates. Logged as a decision.
- Phase regression max 3: a failing phase gets fixed twice, then escalates
  to the owner instead of looping.
