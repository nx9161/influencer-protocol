# Playbook: Crisis — incident response

Trigger: backlash, a disclosure slip, impersonation, a viral misread, a
platform strike. Speed matters; panic doesn't.

## 1. Assess (minutes)
Seats: Vesper, Compliance Officer.
- What happened, where, how big? Screenshot and timestamp everything.
- Is anyone harmed or deceived? That question comes first.

## 2. Contain
Seats: Compliance Officer (blocking), Publishing Manager.
- Pause scheduled posts. Stop the bleeding before explaining it.
- If the persona deceived anyone — even by omission — the fix starts with
  owning it, in the persona's voice, immediately.

## 3. Respond
Seats: Scriptwriter, Vesper. Owner approves the response before it posts.
- One clear statement. No threads, no defensiveness, no deleting
  criticism unless it's abuse.
- AI identity re-disclosed plainly if that was the gap.

## 4. Review
Seats: all relevant.
- Root cause: which gate failed? Fix the gate, not just the incident.
- Logged as a decision. The playbook that failed gets updated.

## Standing rules
- Never fake engagement to drown criticism.
- Never blame the audience.
- The owner's call on anything legal. When in doubt, pause and ask.
